Privacy Policy

Coffee Chat Agent helps users find public professional profiles, prepare personalized outreach, send user-approved emails, maintain a Google Sheets CRM, and detect replies. This policy explains what data the application handles and how it is protected.

Information the application handles

  • Google account information: your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile image when available.
  • Gmail data: authorization to send messages you explicitly approve and limited access needed to identify replies to outreach sent through the application. The application may store Gmail message or thread identifiers, reply timestamps, sender addresses, and short reply snippets.
  • Google Sheets and Drive data: authorization to create and update the Coffee Chat Agent CRM spreadsheet in your Google account.
  • Outreach information: search criteria, public professional-profile results, email addresses, drafts, approval statuses, sending history, and the personal context you choose to provide for drafting.
  • Provider credentials: API keys you choose to add for services such as Tavily, Hunter, Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic.
  • Resume data: a resume file you choose to upload and the factual profile information extracted from it for your review.
  • Security and operational records: session, rate-limit, request, and audit records used to operate and secure the service. Secrets and message bodies are not intentionally written to application logs.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the features you request:

  • authenticate you and keep each user's workspace separate;
  • find public professional-profile results;
  • enrich contact information and generate drafts;
  • analyze an uploaded resume, populate profile suggestions, and attach the resume only when you select that option for a specific email;
  • send only the exact emails you approve;
  • create and update your CRM spreadsheet;
  • detect replies and update outreach status; and
  • prevent abuse, duplicate sends, and unauthorized access.

Google API data

Coffee Chat Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models by Coffee Chat Agent.

Third-party services

The application uses Cloudflare to host the service and store application data. When you activate a feature, the minimum necessary information is sent to the relevant provider: Google for authentication, Gmail, Drive, and Sheets; Tavily for public-web discovery; Hunter for email enrichment; and your selected AI provider for drafting or resume analysis. PDF resume analysis uses the user's configured Gemini credential. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Storage and security

  • Traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • User API keys and Google refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Resume files are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being stored in segmented Cloudflare D1 records.
  • The master encryption key is stored separately as a Cloudflare Worker secret.
  • Credential encryption is bound to the user, credential type, and provider.
  • Plaintext credentials are not returned to the browser after they are saved.
  • Session tokens are stored server-side as cryptographic hashes, and security controls include secure cookies, CSRF protection, same-origin checks, rate limits, and restrictive browser headers.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their Google and provider accounts with strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained while needed to provide the service and maintain the user's outreach history. Provider API keys and uploaded resumes can be deleted in the application's Settings page. Google access can be revoked through Google Account permissions. To request deletion of your account data, encrypted credentials, Google token, prospects, drafts, and audit records, email evanastonlowe@gmail.com from the Google account associated with the workspace. Verified deletion requests will be completed within 30 days unless retention is required by law or for security purposes.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal information and Google user data are not sold. Information is shared only with service providers needed to perform a feature you request, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Material revisions will be reflected by the effective date above. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to evanastonlowe@gmail.com.
