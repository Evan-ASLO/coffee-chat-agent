App Privacy Terms

Effective October 6, 2026

Privacy Policy

Internship & Coffee Chat publishes normalized internship listings and helps signed-in users track applications, find public professional profiles, prepare personalized outreach, send user-approved emails, maintain a Google Sheets CRM, and detect replies. This policy explains what data the application handles and how it is protected.

Information the application handles

  • Job listing information: public internship data such as company, title, location, category, posting date, description, and source URL supplied by the Internship Tracker publisher.
  • Application tracking information: the jobs you save, application stages you select, optional notes, one-time legacy tracker imports, and links between jobs and outreach prospects. This information is scoped to your signed-in account.
  • Private-beta access information: when the beta gate is enabled, a verified Google email address, Google account subject identifier while a request is pending, request status, and request timestamps. The subject identifier is cleared when a request is approved or denied. Unapproved requests do not create a normal application account or session.
  • Google account information: your Google account identifier, email address, name, and profile image when available.
  • Gmail data: authorization to send messages you explicitly approve and limited access needed to identify replies to outreach sent through the application. The application may store Gmail message or thread identifiers, reply timestamps, sender addresses, and short reply snippets.
  • Google Sheets and Drive data: authorization to create and update the Coffee Chat Agent CRM spreadsheet in your Google account.
  • Outreach information: search criteria, public professional-profile results, email addresses, drafts, approval statuses, sending history, and the personal context you choose to provide for drafting.
  • Provider credentials: API keys you choose to add for services such as Tavily, Hunter, Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic.
  • Resume data: a resume file you choose to upload and the factual profile information extracted from it for your review.
  • Tutorial progress: the optional tutorial's status, current step, version, revision, and last-save time, stored for your authenticated account so you can resume across devices. It contains no API keys, profile text, resume contents, or outreach data.
  • Security and operational records: session, rate-limit, request, and audit records used to operate and secure the service, provide your account-scoped activity view, and support restricted owner operational-activity administration. Secrets and message bodies are not intentionally written to application logs.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the features you request:

  • authenticate you and keep each user's workspace separate;
  • remember optional tutorial progress, skipping, and completion;
  • review, approve, deny, revoke, and re-approve private-beta access;
  • publish and filter current internship listings;
  • save jobs and synchronize your private application pipeline;
  • find public professional-profile results;
  • enrich contact information and generate drafts;
  • analyze an uploaded resume, populate profile suggestions, and attach the resume only when you select that option for a specific email;
  • send only the exact emails you approve;
  • create and update your CRM spreadsheet;
  • detect replies and update outreach status; and
  • show account-scoped application, networking, company, and recent activity counts calculated from your existing records;
  • let configured owners administer the beta using basic registered account identifiers and limited operational event summaries;
  • prevent abuse, duplicate sends, and unauthorized access.

Google API data

Internship & Coffee Chat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models by Internship & Coffee Chat.

Third-party services

The application uses Cloudflare to host the service and store application data. A separate Internship Tracker publisher sends signed, normalized public job snapshots to an internal ingestion endpoint. Adzuna-sourced adverts display a self-hosted official attribution image; the image does not cause the browser to contact Adzuna. Opening the attribution or a source job link navigates to the relevant external site under that site's privacy policy. When you activate a feature, the minimum necessary information is sent to the relevant provider: Google for authentication, Gmail, Drive, and Sheets; Tavily for public-web discovery; Hunter for email enrichment; and your selected AI provider for drafting or resume analysis. PDF resume analysis uses the user's configured Gemini credential. If you explicitly select cited Gemini, OpenAI, or Anthropic people search, the application may send only public job-networking criteria (company, professional role/topics, location, approved source domains, and result count) to that selected provider using your encrypted provider key and configured model. It does not send your profile, resume, email, drafts, personal context, or credentials in that prompt. Only one provider is called, after Tavily is insufficient, and direct source citations are required. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

The Analytics page and owner activity dashboard do not use a third-party analytics or advertising service. The personal Analytics page runs bounded, account-scoped queries over records already stored to provide the workspace. Decorative interface crops and the Three.js Career Journey runtime are self-hosted and do not contact an image-generation service or runtime CDN.

Restricted owner operational activity

Only owners configured in the server's beta-administrator allowlist can access the owner dashboard. It shows non-deleted registered accounts' names, email addresses, account identifiers and registration times, recorded action counts, and a paginated feed of event types and timestamps for a selected recent 7- or 28-day period. Examples include people searches, job saves, application changes, draft actions, sends, and beta-access decisions. Where already recorded, only allowlisted search-fallback provider, outcome and error-reason categories are shown. This view does not expose API keys or key hints, tokens or session hashes, resumes, private profile text, draft or email bodies, recipient details, search queries, raw provider responses, or raw audit metadata.

The dashboard adds no pageview tracking, keystroke or screen recording, browser fingerprinting, or live polling. Recorded events are not visits or exact login times. Background work is not evidence that a user was online, and the dashboard does not infer live presence or session duration. No events in a selected period means only that no activity was recorded in that period, not that the account was never used. Not every operation or failure is recorded; historical activity is not reconstructed. Ordinary users cannot access other accounts' owner-dashboard records.

Storage and security

  • Traffic is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • User API keys and Google refresh tokens are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Resume files are encrypted using AES-256-GCM before being stored in segmented Cloudflare D1 records.
  • The master encryption key is stored separately as a Cloudflare Worker secret.
  • Credential encryption is bound to the user, credential type, and provider.
  • Plaintext credentials are not returned to the browser after they are saved.
  • Session tokens are stored server-side as cryptographic hashes, and security controls include secure cookies, CSRF protection, same-origin checks, rate limits, and restrictive browser headers.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their Google and provider accounts with strong passwords and multi-factor authentication.

Retention and deletion

The owner dashboard's 7- and 28-day display windows are query limits, not deletion schedules. Audit records do not currently have a fixed automatic expiry. Existing retention and verified deletion handling below still apply; this dashboard creates no new retention guarantee.

Data is retained while needed to provide the service and maintain the user's application and outreach history. Job listings that disappear from a publisher snapshot are marked inactive; a signed-in user's state may retain a reference so their history remains understandable. Provider API keys and uploaded resumes can be deleted in the application's Settings page. Google access can be revoked through Google Account permissions. To request deletion of your account data, encrypted credentials, Google token, tutorial progress, saved jobs, application states, prospects, drafts, and audit records, email evanastonlowe@gmail.com from the Google account associated with the workspace. Verified deletion requests will be completed within 30 days unless retention is required by law or for security purposes.

Pending or denied beta-access requests may be retained long enough to avoid duplicate active requests, support review, and prevent abuse. A requester may use the same contact address to request deletion.

Sharing and sale of data

Personal information and Google user data are not sold. Information is shared only with service providers needed to perform a feature you request, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the service.

Children's privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and it is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Material revisions will be reflected by the effective date above. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to evanastonlowe@gmail.com.
